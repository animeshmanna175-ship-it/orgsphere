Privacy Policy

App: OrgSphere: Org Chart Maker (com.visuolabs.orgsphere)
Developer: VisuoLabs
Effective Date: November 25, 2026

VisuoLabs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how OrgSphere: Org Chart Maker collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application.

1. Information You Provide & Storage Modes

Local Device Mode (No Sign-In Required): You can use OrgSphere without creating an account. In Local Device Mode, your organization charts, member profiles (names, job titles, departments, locations, reporting lines, compensation figures, OKR scores, and selected avatar photos), and free export counters are stored privately on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers unless you choose to sign in.

Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in with Google to enable cross-device cloud backup and sync, we collect and process:

  • Account Information: Your Google account unique identifier (UID), display name, and email address via Firebase Authentication.
  • Organization Chart Content: The charts, employee nodes, and hierarchy metadata you create or import while signed in, stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore scoped strictly to your authenticated account.

2. Files, Photos, Exports & Printing

  • Profile Photos: When adding a member avatar, OrgSphere uses the Android Photo Picker so the app only accesses the specific image you select—never your broader photo library.
  • CSV Import: When importing a CSV spreadsheet, OrgSphere uses Android's system document picker and only reads the specific file you select to build your org chart.
  • Exports & Direct Printing: PNG images, PDF reports, and MP4 videos are rendered locally on your device. Saved exports use Android scoped storage (MediaStore), sharing uses Android's Share Sheet, and direct printing uses the native Android Print Framework.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

OrgSphere offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (montlyorg and yearlyorg) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, view, or store your payment card details or billing address. We only receive purchase status and subscription entitlement confirmation from Google Play to unlock Pro features and restore purchases.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide core org chart creation, editing, search, analytics, CSV import, export, and printing features.
  • Sync and back up your workspaces across devices when you sign in with Google.
  • Verify and maintain active Google Play subscription entitlements and free-tier export quotas.
  • Maintain app security, prevent unauthorized access, and troubleshoot crashes or errors.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and we do not display third-party advertisements. OrgSphere relies solely on trusted Google infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (optional cloud sign-in and workspace sync)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app subscription processing)

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles data for these services.

6. Data Security

All cloud communications are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud Firestore security rules restrict read and write access strictly to the authenticated account owner. Local device data is stored within Android's private application sandbox.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete charts or members inside the app at any time, or remove all local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling OrgSphere.
  • Cloud Account & Data Deletion: You can delete individual charts inside the app or sign out at any time. 8. Children's Privacy

OrgSphere is a business and productivity tool not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
